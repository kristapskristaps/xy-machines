# Grogu drummer

The display uses the layered vector scene generated by `python artwork/drummer.py`.
It preserves the shared control header and all 18 drum emblems, placing the
played drum's emblem on the front kick head like a band's logo. A separate
vector label shows the selected drum and style, without surrounding the emblem
with a family outline. The generated pose
table keeps each head/arm/stick/kit layer attached to the same local pivot.
The contact table solves each shoulder–hand–stick chain against its playing
surface. Independent shoulder and wrist springs provide follow-through;
forward kinematics keeps the stick at the hand throughout its rebound.

The character follows the user's cartoon reference: a round face, long curved
ears and a wrapped robe, with Wayfarer-style sunglasses and a small smile.
The wide cheeks, smooth forehead and tiny nose keep the face baby-like. Paths
are authored here as native-compatible vectors, with no raster dependency.
The scene stays monochrome apart from small blue/red details on the drum kit.
