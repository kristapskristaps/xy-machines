Play before you build.

Open a synth or drum machine, choose a preset, and use the on-screen keys or your computer keyboard. Sound starts when you play. The animated screen and sound engine come from the machine’s own code.

Put together your own XY.

Each machine includes its source, controls and build instructions. Use the Open-XY SDK to make a firmware selection that fits your instrument and preserves your saved machine numbers.

This is a development collection. Browser auditions use a simplified amplitude gate; stock envelopes, effects, mono/legato behavior and hardware CPU still need device testing. Source availability is separate from release qualification.
